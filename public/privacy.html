Privacy Policy

My AI Chatbot (Orion) · Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version

  • You don’t need an account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your messages and photos are sent to Google’s Gemini AI to create replies.
  • Your saved chats stay on your own device. Our server does not keep them.
  • We don’t show ads, we don’t sell your data, and we don’t use tracking or analytics tools.

1. About this app

My AI Chatbot (Orion) is a chat app. You type a message, speak, or add a photo, and an AI assistant called Orion writes a reply. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app. In this policy, “we” means the developer of the app. You can reach us using the contact details at the end.

2. Replies are written by AI

Replies are generated by artificial intelligence. They can be wrong, incomplete or inappropriate. Don’t rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions, and please don’t share sensitive personal information (such as passwords, bank details or health records) in the chat.

3. Information we handle

  • Your messages and photos. When you send a message, the conversation is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini service so it can write a reply. If you add a photo, a resized copy is sent too. Our server does not store your conversations or photos.
  • Saved chats on your device. The app saves your chats in your browser’s storage on your own device so you can come back to them. Photos are not saved. We cannot see these chats. You can delete a chat with the ✕ button in the chat menu, or remove everything by clearing this site’s data in your browser or app settings.
  • Voice input. The microphone button uses the speech recognition feature of your browser or device. Depending on your browser, your voice may be processed by its provider (for example, Google in Chrome). We do not receive or store your audio. We only receive the text it produces, which is handled like any message you type.
  • Reports. If you tap “Report” on a reply, we receive that reply, the message it answered, and any reason you type. We use this to review harmful or inappropriate replies. Reports are kept in our server logs and may be forwarded to the developer by email or a messaging service.
  • Technical information. To stop abuse, the server briefly counts how many messages come from the same internet address (IP address). These counts are kept in memory only and are cleared when the server restarts and after a day. Our hosting provider may also keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and request time, as part of running its servers.
  • Settings. Your choice of light or dark mode is saved on your device.

4. How we use information

  • To write replies to your messages and photos.
  • To keep the service secure and limit misuse.
  • To review reports and improve the quality and safety of the app’s replies.

5. Who we share information with

  • Google (Gemini API). Your messages and photos are processed by Google to create replies. This app currently uses Google’s free (unpaid) Gemini service. Under Google’s terms for that service, Google may use the content submitted to it, and the replies, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. For this reason, please avoid sharing sensitive information. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to its handling of that data.
  • Hosting provider. Our server runs on Render, which processes requests on our behalf.
  • Report delivery. Reports may pass through an email or messaging service so they reach the developer.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep information

  • Saved chats stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Our server does not keep conversations or photos after a reply has been sent.
  • Reports are kept in server logs for as long as our hosting provider retains logs, and in the developer’s email or messages until the developer deletes them.
  • Message counters used to limit misuse are cleared when the server restarts and after one day.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete your saved chats at any time, clear this site’s data in your browser or app settings, or stop using the app. Because we don’t have accounts, we usually can’t tell who sent a report. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information about you. If you want to ask about a report you sent or have other questions, contact us and we will do our best to help.

8. Children

This app is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove anything we hold.

9. Security

Information sent between the app, our server and Google travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Other countries

Google and our hosting provider may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, we will update this page.
